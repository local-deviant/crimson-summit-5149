Method & tests

The curve. ln(mcap) = 7.545 + 0.50 × ln(TVL) + category offset, fitted on 71 traded protocols whose token is a claim on their own TVL. The slope 0.50 is a prior, not the OLS slope — the free OLS slope on these comps is 0.26, and across every defensible comp rule it runs 0.21–0.68, so the data cannot pin it down. The intercept and the shrunk offsets (n/(n+8)) are fitted at that slope. Multiple clamped to [0.08, 1.20]; growth factor 1 + clamp(7d change, −60, 120)/100 × 0.3.

Launch odds. A hazard/survival model: odds = [P(ever) − P(ever)·F(a)] / [1 − P(ever)·F(a)]. P(ever) per category is the token rate divided by the mean launch-CDF over the category's ages, shrunk toward the pooled 0.56 with strength 15, capped at [0.05, 0.85]. F(a) is a Weibull (shape 0.80, scale 3000 days) fitted by maximum likelihood on the snapshot's current-status data. The cumulative token rate is not used as odds — it rises with age, which points backwards.

The age term was tested and rejected. With ln(age) in the curve, leave-one-out is x2.77 vs x2.94 without it — but walk-forward is x5.80 vs x2.40. The age pattern is a cohort effect (young test protocols run 2× the train set's multiples) that does not travel forward. The ablation table below shows every variant tried.
